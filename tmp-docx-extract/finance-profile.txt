Company Overview
OPG Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a South African financial services company that provides innovative and tailored financial solutions designed to unlock business potential and address cash flow challenges.
The company offers specialised financial support to Small, Medium and Micro Enterprises (SMMEs), professionals, contractors and organisations operating within both the public and private sectors. OPG Solutions focuses on delivering structured financial solutions, financial administration systems, and funding facilitation that enable businesses to access working capital and successfully deliver projects.
Through advanced financial technology and integrated financial systems, OPG Solutions provides flexible end-to-end financial support for business operations, infrastructure development projects, and procurement activities in South Africa and abroad.
The company is committed to empowering businesses and driving economic growth by improving access to finance, strengthening financial management systems, and providing sustainable funding solutions.

Financial Systems and Technology
OPG Solutions operates modern cloud‑based financial systems and secure financial technology platforms to ensure efficient financial administration, secure transactions and transparent reporting.The company utilizes the Brownstar Cloud Accounting System, an advanced accounting and payroll platform designed to simplify financial management and support high volumes of financial transactions.
Key capabilities include:
Invoicing and quotation management
Customer and supplier account management
Expense tracking
Accounts payable and receivable monitoring
Tax management
Inventory and stock control
Financial reporting
Payroll and payslip processing
OPG Solutions also utilises secure financial transaction infrastructure supported by Altron FinTech solutions, providing PCI‑DSS certified payment and collection platforms, digital payment processing, card disbursement solutions, loan management systems and financial issuance solutions.
